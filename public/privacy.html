🛡️ SentinelVault Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

SentinelVault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension and web services handle user data under a strict Zero-Knowledge security model.

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

SentinelVault operates on a zero-knowledge security model. All vault items, credentials, and passwords are encrypted on your local device using AES-256-GCM encryption before being saved or synchronized. Your Master Password is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Data Collection & Disclosure

We collect minimal data strictly necessary to provide service functionality:

  • Personally Identifiable Information: Your account email address is stored for authentication purposes only.
  • Authentication Information: Encrypted password vault blobs are stored on our servers. Because these blobs are encrypted client-side, we cannot view or access your passwords.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect web browsing history, site analytics, or page visits.
  • We do NOT track mouse movements, keystrokes, or user behavior.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or transfer your personal data to third parties.
  • We do NOT use data for advertising or credit evaluation.

4. Extension Permissions Usage

  • activeTab & scripting: Used solely to detect login fields and auto-fill credentials on active web pages upon your explicit request.
  • storage: Used to store encrypted vault data locally on your device for offline availability and save local preferences (e.g. auto-lock timers).
